Cleaning decisions



1. Firstly, I removed the duplicates by using the conditional formatting at the home tab to ensure that the entire rows are not read more than once in order to give correct results.



2\. To deal with the missing values, I clicked find and select, go to special and blanks to show the missing values. Rather than deleting the entire rows, I replaced the missing values in the segment column with unknown and I also compared the products with missing values with other products in order to estimate the unit price to ensure that the resulting revenue would be accurate.



3\. To deal with the negative quantities, I first investigated them to ensure that they didn't represent cancelled transactions and finally deleted the rows since they represented incorrect data.
