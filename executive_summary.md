&#x20;Key Insights



&#x20;1. Revenue changed significantly from month to month



Monthly revenue varied substantially between January and August 2026. May was the strongest month, generating GH₵602,685.75, while August was the weakest at GH₵316,055.00.



The largest increase occurred in May, when revenue increased by 65.03% compared with April. The largest decline occurred in June, when revenue fell by 42.04% compared with May. Revenue also fell by 39.22% in August compared with July.



This shows that sales performance was not consistent throughout the period and should be monitored closely.



&#x20;2. Greater Accra generated the most revenue



Greater Accra generated GH₵783,853.00, representing 22.67% of total revenue. Western was the second-highest region, generating GH₵683,810.75, or 19.77%.



Together, Greater Accra and Western contributed 42.44% of total revenue. However, all six regions contributed meaningfully to overall sales.



This indicates that Greater Accra and Western are the strongest-performing regions, while the business still has a relatively broad regional customer base.



&#x20;3. Computer products were the main source of revenue



The Computers category generated GH₵2,164,050.00, making it the highest-revenue category.



The Performance Laptop was the highest-revenue product, generating GH₵1,052,880.00 from 129 units. The Office Laptop generated GH₵769,860.00 from 148 units.



These results show that higher-value computer products are major contributors to overall revenue.



&#x20;4. Selling more units did not always mean generating more revenue



Accessories had the highest number of units sold, with 758 units, but generated GH₵151,598.00 in revenue.



In comparison, Computers sold 388 units but generated GH₵2,164,050.00.



At the individual product level, the Mechanical Keyboard sold 199 units and generated GH₵47,880.00, while the Performance Laptop sold 129 units and generated GH₵1,052,880.00.



This shows that sales volume and revenue can tell different stories about product performance.



&#x20;5. Customer value differed significantly



Ibrahim Tetteh was the highest-revenue customer, generating GH₵102,539.50. Kojo Boateng followed closely with GH₵102,346.75.



The top 10 customers accounted for approximately 22.75% of total revenue.



The number of purchases also did not always indicate how valuable a customer was. Priscilla Issah made 15 purchases but generated GH₵22,943.00, with an average purchase value of GH₵1,529.53.



In comparison, Kojo Sarpong made 12 purchases but generated GH₵62,199.00, with an average purchase value of GH₵5,183.25.



This suggests that management should consider both how often customers purchase and how much they spend.



&#x20;6. Salespeople differed in both sales and order value



Francis Amoah generated the highest salesperson revenue at GH₵699,100.50 and also recorded the highest average purchase value at GH₵4,315.44.



Benjamin Osei handled the highest number of orders, with 176, but generated GH₵558,175.50.



This shows that handling more orders does not necessarily result in the highest revenue. The value of each sale is also important.



&#x20;7. Customers used different payment methods relatively evenly



Cash was the most frequently used payment method, with 277 orders, representing 27.70% of all orders.



Bank Transfer accounted for 25.30%, Mobile Money for 23.80%, and Card payments for 23.20%.



The results show that customers use a range of payment methods rather than relying heavily on one option.



&#x20;Recommendations



&#x20;1. Investigate the large changes in monthly sales



Management should investigate the reasons behind the major changes in monthly revenue, particularly the 42.04% decline in June and the 39.22% decline in August.



Understanding these changes can help management improve sales planning and prepare for periods of weaker performance.



&#x20;2. Focus on high-value products while reviewing weaker products



Management should continue supporting strong revenue-generating products such as the Performance Laptop and Office Laptop.



At the same time, lower-revenue products such as the Wireless Mouse, which generated GH₵9,945.00 from 117 units, should be reviewed to understand their performance and determine whether changes in pricing, promotion, or product strategy are needed.



&#x20;3. Strengthen relationships with high-value customers and improve sales practices



Management should identify and retain high-value customers while studying successful sales practices.



For example, Kojo Sarpong generated GH₵62,199.00 from 12 purchases, while Priscilla Issah generated GH₵22,943.00 from 15 purchases. This demonstrates the importance of increasing the value of each customer purchase, not simply increasing the number of purchases.



The business could also examine the sales practices associated with Francis Amoah's GH₵699,100.50 in revenue and GH₵4,315.44 average purchase value.



&#x20;Data Limitations



1\. The analysis covers January to August 2026 only. It does not include sales from the remaining months of the year.



2\. The analysis shows patterns and differences in sales performance but does not explain why those differences occurred. Further investigation would be needed to identify the specific reasons behind changes in sales.



3\. The results depend on the accuracy and completeness of the recorded sales, customer, product, and payment information. Errors or missing information in the underlying records could affect the results.
