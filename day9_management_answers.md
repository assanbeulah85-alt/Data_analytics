&#x20;8. What are the three most important management actions supported by the evidence?



&#x20;Action 1: Investigate revenue volatility



Revenue fluctuated substantially between months. May generated the highest revenue at GH₵602,685.75, while August generated the lowest at GH₵316,055.00. June experienced the largest month-on-month decline of 42.04%.



Management should investigate the factors behind these large fluctuations before setting future sales targets and forecasts.



&#x20;Action 2: Prioritize high-value products and review weaker products



Computer products generated GH₵2,164,050.00, making Computers the strongest category by revenue. The Performance Laptop alone generated GH₵1,052,880.00.



At the other end, the Wireless Mouse generated only GH₵9,945.00 from 117 units, with revenue per unit of GH₵85.00.



Management should prioritize products that generate strong revenue while reviewing the performance, pricing, and demand of weaker products.



&#x20;Action 3: Focus on high-value customers and sales patterns



Customer results show that order frequency does not necessarily indicate customer value. Priscilla Issah had 15 orders but an AOV of only GH₵1,529.53, while Kojo Sarpong had 12 orders with an AOV of GH₵5,183.25.



Francis Amoah also generated the highest salesperson revenue and AOV, at GH₵699,100.50 and GH₵4,315.44 respectively.



Management should identify high-value customer and sales patterns, strengthen customer retention efforts, and examine successful sales practices that could increase average order value.



&#x20;Overall Conclusion



The analysis shows that revenue performance is highly variable across months, regions, products, customers, and salespeople. Greater Accra is the strongest region, Computers are the strongest product category by revenue, and the Performance Laptop is the highest-revenue product.



The analysis also shows that high sales volume does not always mean high revenue. Accessories recorded the highest number of units, while some customers with fewer orders generated substantially higher revenue per order.



Management should therefore focus on understanding revenue volatility, strengthening high-value product and customer performance, and investigating unusual differences in customer and product purchasing patterns.
